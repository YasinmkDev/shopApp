Privacy Policy

App: Shopkeeper POS (com.shahabkhan34.ShopkeeperApp)

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Summary: Shopkeeper POS ("we", "us", or "our") is an offline-first Point of Sale and bookkeeping tool designed for retail storekeepers. Your business data belongs solely to you. We do not sell your personal data or your customers' transaction data to third parties.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Shopkeeper POS collects, stores, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related cloud backup services. By using Shopkeeper POS, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo URL provided by Google via Firebase Authentication. If you use the app in Guest Mode, no account information is collected.

B. Shop & Merchant Profile

You may optionally provide store details such as your shop name (in English and Urdu), store contact phone number, physical address, and optional payment receiver details (such as your EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank account identifier for customer billing displays).

C. Inventory & Transaction Records

To provide core POS register capabilities, the app stores:

  • Product catalog (product names, barcodes, purchase cost, retail selling prices, stock quantities, and product photos).
  • Sales receipts and transaction histories.
  • Customer Khata (credit ledger) entries: customer name, phone number, and outstanding credit balances. This information is entered by you strictly for your shop's credit bookkeeping.

D. Device & Storage Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Used exclusively when you choose to take or select photos of products or your shop logo.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to check network connectivity and synchronize data to Google Firebase or Google Drive when enabled.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide local and offline point-of-sale billing and inventory calculations.
  • Maintain your customer credit (Udhaar Khata) balances and generate printable receipt vouchers.
  • Sync your store records securely to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so you never lose your data across devices.
  • Back up database snapshots and product images to your personal Google Drive (when authorized by you).

4. Third-Party Services & Cloud Infrastructure

Shopkeeper POS integrates with trusted service providers to deliver cloud backup services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage): Used for secure user authentication and cloud synchronization. All data transferred between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.
  • Google Drive API: If you connect Google Drive, data and image backups are stored in your private Google Drive account. We do not expose your Google Drive files to the public.

5. Data Security

We apply robust security safeguards to protect your business records:

  • All cloud communications utilize HTTPS with TLS 1.2+ encryption.
  • Firestore and Storage rules enforce strict tenant isolation: each merchant can only read and write data belonging to their verified Firebase User ID (`shops/{userId}`).
  • Offline records are stored in private app sandboxed storage on your device.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active.

How to Delete Your Account and Data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, business profile, inventory, sales, and Khata records at any time by going to Settings > Google Cloud Backup > Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  • Web Deletion Portal: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request account deletion online at Account Deletion Portal.

On a successful in-app deletion, records under your Firebase account (shops/{userId} in Firestore and Firebase Storage) and your Firebase Authentication profile are permanently purged. If Storage cleanup fails, deletion is aborted so you can retry. Web portal requests are handled manually (usually within about 48 hours). Google Drive OAuth access is revoked on successful in-app deletion; any files already saved in your personal Google Drive remain under your control and are not deleted by the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Shopkeeper POS is intended for business owners and retail operators. It is not directed at or designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
